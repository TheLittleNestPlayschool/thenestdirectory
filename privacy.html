The Little Nest Playschool

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

The Little Nest Playschool ("The Little Nest", "we", "us") respects the privacy of parents, guardians, children and other people who contact us. This notice explains how we handle personal information through our Facebook Pages and Messenger conversations, inquiries, advertisements, registration and trial or visit forms, our Ads Journey administration system, and related services.

1. Information we receive

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect or receive:

  • Your name, contact details and Facebook or Messenger identifiers made available through the platform.
  • Messages you send to our Pages and the responses from our team, including message dates, attachments, photos or videos you choose to share.
  • Information you provide about a child, such as name, age, preferred branch or class schedule, and details needed to answer an inquiry or arrange a visit, free trial or enrollment.
  • Information entered in forms, including contact and scheduling details, and information needed to manage an enrollment if you proceed.
  • Information about how an inquiry reached us, such as the Facebook Page or advertisement and referral information provided by Meta.
  • Basic technical information associated with our websites or forms, where collected by the services used to deliver them.

We ask parents and guardians to share only information needed for the inquiry or service. Please avoid sending sensitive records or unnecessary personal details through Messenger.

2. How and why we use information

We use relevant information to respond to questions; discuss our playschool programs; arrange and confirm visits or free trials; manage enrollment-related communication; connect you with the appropriate branch; provide administrative support; maintain records of inquiries and follow-ups; protect our services; and understand which advertising and inquiry processes are effective.

Where appropriate, software tools may help organize conversations, recognize questions, summarize inquiries, identify follow-up needs, and prepare suggested replies. These tools may include AI-assisted processing. Our staff review and send parent-facing Messenger replies; automated suggestions do not independently decide whether a child is accepted or enrolled.

We process information as appropriate to respond to your request, take steps toward services you ask about, carry out our legitimate operational interests where permitted, comply with legal obligations, or act on consent when required.

3. Who may access information

Information may be accessed by authorized members of The Little Nest's central administrative team and, when relevant, personnel at the playschool branch associated with your inquiry or enrollment. Franchise operators receive information appropriate to their role in providing or managing the service.

We also use service providers that support messaging, data storage, web hosting, security, forms and AI-assisted administration. These can include Meta, our application and database providers, and contracted technology processors. They receive or process information only as relevant to the services they provide and subject to applicable safeguards. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect legitimate rights and safety. We do not sell personal information collected from Messenger inquiries.

4. Children's information

Parents and guardians generally provide information about their children when asking about playschool services. Our parent inquiry and scheduling processes are intended for adults, not for children to use independently. We use children's information for the educational, enrollment and family-service purposes described here and limit access to personnel with a relevant role.

5. Storage, security and retention

We use administrative and technical measures intended to protect information and limit access to authorized personnel. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Our central systems and service providers may process data using infrastructure outside the Philippines, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

We keep inquiry and conversation information while needed to respond, manage follow-ups, support enrollment and related services, maintain operational records, resolve disputes, or meet legal duties. When information is no longer needed for those purposes, we delete or de-identify it as appropriate. Certain records may need to be retained where the law permits or requires.

6. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access or correct personal information, object to or withdraw consent for certain processing, and request deletion or other action concerning your information. We may need to verify that a request comes from the person entitled to make it before acting on it.

7. How to request deletion of Messenger or inquiry data

Email admin@thelittle-nest.com with the subject Data Deletion Request. Please tell us your name, the Facebook Page or playschool branch you contacted, and approximately when you sent the messages, so we can locate the relevant records. Do not send passwords or identity documents unless we specifically explain why additional verification is necessary.

We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable privacy law. If information must be retained for a valid legal reason, we will explain that where appropriate. Deleting records from our systems does not itself delete a conversation or other information held independently by Facebook or Messenger; you can manage that through Meta's own privacy controls.

8. Other websites and services

Our Pages, websites, forms and messages may link to services operated by Meta or other providers. Their own privacy practices apply to information they collect independently. You can review Meta's privacy information through its platforms.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when revisions are published.
